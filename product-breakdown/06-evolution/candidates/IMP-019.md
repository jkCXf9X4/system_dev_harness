## Theme

PBS / Decomposition — Extend the informational WBS from IMP-016 into a project management WBS with estimation and scheduling

## Evidence

- IMP-016 WBS mapping (workflow stages as activities); INCOSE §2.3.4.1 describing WBS derivation from FBS/PBS; current absence of any project management WBS artifact

## Current Pain Or Risk

No project management WBS exists for effort estimation, scheduling, or resource planning. The informational WBS in IMP-016 identifies work activities but is not structured for project planning and does not include estimates or dependencies.

## Proposed Improvement

Create a project management WBS artifact that maps work packages to FBS and PBS elements, includes effort estimates, dependencies, and a suggested schedule for implementation projects.

## Expected Benefit

Enables effort estimation and scheduling for future implementation work; provides a project management view of the decomposition model; bridges the gap between decomposition design and execution planning.

## Risk And Blast Radius

Low — informational artifact only. Risk of scope creep if treated as an authoritative project plan. Must clearly state it is a planning aid, not a commitment. Should be created after IMP-016 is complete.
