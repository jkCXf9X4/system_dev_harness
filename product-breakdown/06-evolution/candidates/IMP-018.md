## Theme

PBS / Decomposition — Align product-breakdown guidance files with the FBS-PBS-WBS decomposition model from INCOSE §2.3.4.1

## Evidence

- IMP-016 FBS-PBS-WBS mapping; current guidance files under .opencode/dev_harness/product-breakdown/ (templates, workflow policies) that do not reference FBS, PBS, or WBS concepts

## Current Pain Or Risk

Guidance files treat the product breakdown as a flat artifact collection without distinguishing functional, physical, and work decomposition layers. Contributors following guidance may not understand where their work fits in the decomposition model, leading to inconsistent artifact placement.

## Proposed Improvement

Review and update .opencode/dev_harness/product-breakdown/ guidance files (templates, policies, instructions) to incorporate FBS-PBS-WBS terminology, layer distinctions, and traceability expectations.

## Expected Benefit

Guidance that teaches the decomposition model; consistent contributor understanding of functional vs. physical vs. work artifacts; reduced ambiguity in artifact placement.

## Risk And Blast Radius

Medium — guidance changes affect all future contributor behavior; must not contradict existing workflow policies; requires maintainer review. Coordinate with IMP-016 completion.
