## Theme

PBS / Decomposition — Gap analysis and completion of the FBS and PBS trees identified in IMP-016

## Evidence

- IMP-016 FBS mapping (UC-001 through UC-014, PC-001 through PC-010) and PBS mapping (agent hierarchy, artifact tree); potential gaps where functional requirements lack corresponding physical artifacts or vice versa

## Current Pain Or Risk

The FBS and PBS trees documented in IMP-016 may have missing nodes — functional requirements without physical implementations, or physical artifacts without traceable functional origins. Incomplete trees weaken traceability and reduce confidence that all requirements are addressed.

## Proposed Improvement

Perform a gap analysis comparing the FBS tree (use cases, commitments) against the PBS tree (agents, artifacts); identify missing nodes; propose new use cases, commitments, or artifacts to fill verified gaps.

## Expected Benefit

Complete traceability from functional requirements through physical design; no orphaned artifacts or untraced functions; improved confidence that all requirements have implementation coverage.

## Risk And Blast Radius

Medium — scope could expand significantly if many gaps are found; new artifacts must integrate with existing structure; requires maintainer agreement on what constitutes a genuine gap vs. intentional omission. Depends on IMP-016 completion.
