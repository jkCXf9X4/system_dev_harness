## Theme

Design a clean responsibility separation between the validation stage and the reviewer stage for VAL-001 through VAL-005 criteria checking, eliminating the belt-and-suspenders overlap accepted in IMP-026.

## Evidence

- `orchestrator-validation.md`: references VAL-001 through VAL-005 from `acceptance-criteria.md` for the upstream advisory check.
- `orchestrator-reviewer.md` line 75: independently references VAL-001 through VAL-005 from `acceptance-criteria.md` as part of the final gate check.
- Source: BC-003 from IMP-026 reviewer findings; documented as deferred follow-up in IMP-026 plan summary (Risks section, item #1, lines 113-114).
- Plan file: `.opencode/dev_harness_plans/2026-06-30_160000-IMP-026.md`

## Current Pain Or Risk

Both stages independently check the same VAL-001 through VAL-005 criteria. This creates a KM-004 (parallel solutions) risk where two stages have overlapping responsibility for the same concern. Without clear separation:
1. Validation and reviewer findings may diverge over time, creating confusion about which stage's interpretation is authoritative.
2. Future maintainers may update one stage's criteria reference but forget the other.
3. The redundancy is opaque to operators — two stages checking the same things without documented rationale.

## Proposed Improvement

Design a clean responsibility separation:
- **Validation (upstream advisory)**: Performs the intent-to-outcome traceability check and user-need satisfaction assessment using VAL-001 through VAL-005. This is the advisory first-pass check where gaps can be caught early.
- **Reviewer (final gate)**: Performs a meta-check — verifies that validation was performed and its findings are complete and sound. Does not independently re-run VAL-001 through VAL-005.
- Remove VAL-001 through VAL-005 criteria text from the reviewer prompt and replace with a meta-check instruction.
- Update `acceptance-criteria.md` to clarify ownership boundaries for each criterion.

## Expected Benefit

- Eliminates KM-004 (parallel solutions) risk.
- Clear, single-owner responsibility for each validation criterion.
- Reviewer focus shifts from redundant checking to meta-verification of validation completeness.
- Reduced maintenance burden — only one stage needs updating when criteria change.
- Preserves the belt-and-suspenders safety net through the meta-check mechanism rather than through duplicate work.

## Risk And Blast Radius

Medium — changes to both `orchestrator-validation.md` and `orchestrator-reviewer.md`, plus potential updates to `acceptance-criteria.md` to clarify ownership. The reviewer meta-check must be carefully designed so it does not accidentally drop coverage during the transition. Validation must be mature enough to be the single authoritative checker before the reviewer drops its independent check.
