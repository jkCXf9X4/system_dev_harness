## Evidence

- `plan-summary-schema.md` line 16 — `plan_approval_status` defined as `not_required` or `pending` only
- `control-policy.md` lines 56–83 — Plan Draft Approval section covers planner→builder routing but has no lifecycle mention after that handoff

## Current Pain Or Risk

`plan_approval_status` stays "pending" forever even after reviewer approves and work completes. The schema only defines `not_required` and `pending` states with no mechanism to record approval or completion. Control-policy.md describes the draft approval routing (approve/revise/reject) but never defines which stage updates the status to reflect actual lifecycle state. This creates a traceability gap where plan summaries remain semantically incomplete after delivery.

## Proposed Improvement

Add `approved` and `completed` states to `plan_approval_status`, or add a separate `gate_approval_status` field. Update control-policy.md to define which stage updates the status and under what conditions.

## Expected Benefit

Plan summaries reflect actual approval/completion state. Downstream readers (reporter, reflection, future planners) can distinguish plans that are pending, approved, or fully delivered.

## Risk And Blast Radius

Low — two workflow policy files. Schema addition + stage responsibility definition. No change to routing logic, approval gates, or agent behavior.
