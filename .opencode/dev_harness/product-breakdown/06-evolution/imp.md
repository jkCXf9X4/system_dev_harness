### Key Observations

1. **13 of 25 files (52%) have zero improvement-discovery language.** Most of these are specialist helper agents (discovery, contract, architecture, researcher, lessons, memory, memory-curator, build-error-resolver, verifier, review-completeness) and two workflow policies (information-hygiene, review-output). These files perform their assigned function without any instruction to notice or surface improvement opportunities.

2. **5 files are correctly designed as High.** The dedicated improvement stages (`orchestrator-improvement`, `orchestrator-improvement-evaluator`), the two improvement discovery prompts, and the control policy's improvement evaluation section all have strong encouragement.

3. **The gap is in the "None" files.** Most specialist helpers could benefit from a lightweight instruction to surface improvement opportunities they encounter during their normal work, without changing their primary function.

4. **No Stage 2 files were modified.** This deliverable only touches `product-breakdown/06-evolution/imp.md`.
