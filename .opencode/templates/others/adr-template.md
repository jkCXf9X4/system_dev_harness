# Architecture Decision Record (ADR) Template

Use this template when a task requires a durable architectural decision that should be reviewable and traceable in the repository.

```text
Title: [Short descriptive title]
Status: [proposed | accepted | superseded | rejected]

Context:
[Why the decision was needed. What problem, constraint, or opportunity prompted it?]

Options Considered:
[2-3 alternatives that were seriously evaluated, with a brief note on each.]

Decision:
[The chosen approach, stated clearly enough to stand on its own.]

Consequences:
[Tradeoffs, constraints, follow-up work, or risks created by the decision.]

Traceability:
[Link or reference the intent, product commitment, architecture, implementation, and verification artifacts this ADR satisfies.]
```

Best-practice notes:
- Use the template for decisions that change boundaries, responsibilities, contracts, structure, or long-lived conventions.
- Keep the ADR focused on one decision or a tightly related decision set.
- Reference the repository's existing intent, architecture, and implementation artifacts rather than introducing a separate narrative chain.
