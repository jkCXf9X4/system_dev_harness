# 06 Evolution Layer

The evolution layer describes how the product and system are expected to change over time.

## Typical Artifacts



```text
roadmap.md   - outlines planned future work and sequencing
backlog.md   - captures deferred or unscheduled work items
risks.md     - records known risks, tradeoffs, and open concerns
changelog.md - summarizes notable changes over time
decisions/   - stores evolution-level decisions and rationale
```

## Questions Answered

- What is planned next?
- What risks are known?
- What changes have been made?
- What tradeoffs may need to be revisited?
- What future capabilities or architectural changes are anticipated?

## Example Decisions

```text
ED-001-defer-enterprise-permissions-until-v2.md
ED-002-plan-for-external-integrations-after-core-workflows.md
ED-003-revisit-reporting-architecture-after-usage-growth.md
```

Evolution decisions are often temporary or strategic. They should make clear what is intentionally deferred, what is expected to change, and what risks are accepted.
