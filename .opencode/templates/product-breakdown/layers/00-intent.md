# 00 Intent Layer

The intent layer explains why the product exists, who it serves, and what success means.

## Typical Artifacts


```text
purpose.md      - captures the problem statement and why the product exists
users.md        - describes the target users and their needs
outcomes.md     - defines the outcomes that indicate success
constraints.md  - records known limits, constraints, and non-goals
assumptions.md  - lists assumptions that the intent depends on
decisions/      - stores intent-level decisions and rationale
```

## Questions Answered

- Why are we building this?
- Who is it for?
- What outcomes are we trying to create?
- What constraints or assumptions shape the work?

## Example Decisions

```text
ID-001-target-small-internal-teams-first.md
ID-002-optimize-for-fast-onboarding.md
ID-003-prioritize-self-service-usage.md
```

Intent decisions usually affect the whole product and should be made carefully because they shape downstream product, architecture, and implementation choices.
